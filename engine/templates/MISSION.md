## Standing invariants (do not edit)

1. Truth lives on disk. A claim without a persisted artifact is a rumor.
2. Act on the reversible; stop only on the irreversible or genuinely ambiguous.
3. A mismatch is a clue. Investigate WHY before adjusting.
4. The checker is never the doer, and ideally is cold.
5. An honest BLOCKED with a crisp unblock criterion beats a manufactured verdict.
6. Bound your own context. Hand off before you bloat.
7. Read the frozen criterion, not the stale plan.
8. Do not pre-load conclusions into future work.
9. Every out-of-band human word becomes a durable artifact.
10. Escalate scope changes, not task failures.
